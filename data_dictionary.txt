----------------------------------------------------------------------
3. KEY TERMS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
COICOP
  Standard classification of consumer spending. Codes go from general
  to specific: 01 Food & drinks -> 011 Food -> 0111 Bread & cereals.

Index (2015 = 100)
  Price level compared with 2015. 120 = 20% higher than in 2015.

Annual rate of change
  % change compared with the same month one year earlier.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
4. DIFFERENCES BETWEEN THE TWO SOURCES
----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Date format : CSO YYYYMM (integer) vs Eurostat YYYY-MM (text)
2. Categories  : CSO uses codes, Eurostat uses names
3. Measures    : CSO has 5 statistics, Eurostat has only the annual
                 rate of change
